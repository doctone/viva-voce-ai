# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary users are teachers and assessors preparing for, conducting, and reviewing viva-style assessment work. They are usually working in focused academic contexts where they need to move between preparation, reading, evaluation, and record-keeping without unnecessary visual noise or playful distractions.

## Product Purpose
Viva Voce AI exists to support teacher-led assessment workflows around oral examinations and related academic review. The product should help teachers prepare materials, review evidence, run or document assessment sessions, and maintain clear records with less cognitive overhead than generic admin software.

## Brand Personality
Calm, scholarly, authoritative. The product should feel disciplined and legible, closer to a well-edited academic workspace than a gamified education app. Its tone should communicate confidence, professional care, and institutional credibility.

## Anti-references
Do not make this feel like gamified edtech with bright rewards, playful microcopy, or student-app energy. Avoid generic SaaS dashboard tropes that foreground metrics and startup-style polish over concentration. Avoid casual consumer-product aesthetics that make professional assessment work feel lightweight.

## Design Principles
1. Design for concentrated academic work: support long reading, careful judgment, and record clarity over speed theatrics.
2. Project institutional confidence: interfaces should feel trustworthy, durable, and professionally composed.
3. Reduce cognitive noise: every element should help a teacher read, decide, or document with less friction.
4. Keep the teacher in control: workflows should reinforce authority, traceability, and deliberate action.
5. Prefer legibility over novelty: memorable design is welcome, but never at the expense of clarity during assessment tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Assume WCAG AA as the baseline for the product. Prioritize readable typography, dependable contrast, keyboard-accessible workflows, and interaction patterns that remain calm and usable during long assessment sessions.
