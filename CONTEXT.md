# Viva Assessment

Viva Voce AI supports teacher-led oral assessment of a student's submitted work. This glossary establishes the shared language used to discuss that assessment workflow.

## Language

### People and Work

**Teacher**:
The educator who prepares, conducts, and reviews a viva and remains responsible for assessment decisions.
_Avoid_: Examiner, assessor, user

**Student**:
The learner whose submitted work and understanding are examined through a viva.
_Avoid_: Candidate, pupil, subject

**Submission**:
A named body of student work that provides the source material for one viva.
_Avoid_: Coursework, document, answer, student submission

### Viva Preparation

**Viva**:
A teacher-led oral assessment associated with one submission, used to explore the student's understanding and ownership of their work.
_Avoid_: Interview, oral exam, session

**Viva Question**:
A prompt prepared for the teacher to ask during a viva. It may be generated by the system, edited by the teacher, or added manually.
_Avoid_: Prompt, submission question, AI question

**Teacher Note**:
Private guidance attached to a viva question that tells the teacher what evidence or reasoning to listen for.
_Avoid_: Answer, model answer, marking note

**Question Category**:
The line of inquiry represented by a viva question: comprehension and accuracy, argumentation and reasoning, or authenticity and ownership.
_Avoid_: Question type, topic, tag

**Recommended Question**:
A viva question identified as especially useful when the teacher must select a smaller question set.
_Avoid_: Starred question, highlighted question, required question

### Viva Evidence

**Viva Recording**:
An audio record of a viva associated with its submission.
_Avoid_: Submission audio, attachment, playback
